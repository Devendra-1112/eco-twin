# Project Abstract: EcoTwin Smart City Digital Twin Platform

As global environmental challenges intensify, policymakers and researchers require advanced tools to monitor, predict, and optimize sustainability efforts. The **EcoTwin Smart City Digital Twin** is an AI-powered sustainability intelligence platform designed to track and forecast global environmental metrics in real time. Built on a modern, edge-deployable web architecture (Hono, TypeScript, Vanilla CSS) and interactive visualization libraries (Plotly, Three.js), EcoTwin serves as a comprehensive digital twin for global sustainability analysis.

The platform integrates a multi-layered data dashboard with transparent simulation models and genuine external data feeds to provide deep, actionable insights. Key technological components and capabilities include:

- **Predictive Scenario Simulation**: Multi-factor dynamic scenario modeling forecasting 10-year environmental trajectories with physical bounds clamping, confidence envelopes, and deterministic reproducibility.
- **Policy Target Optimization**: Parameterized multi-objective gradient-decay search heuristics to find balanced policy lever combinations that maximize sustainability scores.
- **Environmental Hazard & Anomaly Tracking**: Structured temporal baseline monitoring with severity thresholding to detect critical resource consumption spikes, integrated with live NASA EONET natural event feeds.
- **Explainable Feature Attribution**: Additive linear feature contribution analysis decomposing sustainability scores into individual indicator deltas.
- **Satellite Remote Sensing**: Direct retrieval of NASA GIBS VIIRS 250m true-color optical satellite imagery combined with synthetic computer vision change-detection overlays.
- **UN SDG Progress Tracking**: Continuous monitoring across major Sustainable Development Goals (Clean Water, Clean Energy, Climate Action, Sustainable Cities) with empirical trajectory mapping.
- **Interactive Visual Mapping**: Interactive 3D WebGL globe and choropleth mapping to visually interpret the sustainability status of over 60 countries.
- **Resilient AI Analytics**: Natural language sustainability analyst powered by Google Gemini with automatic multi-key failover and deterministic local telemetry fallback grounding.
- **Data Trust & Integrity Framework**: Five-tier explicit data provenance framework (Live external data, Retrieved external data, Calculated estimate, Heuristic result, Simulated demo data) ensuring total transparency and zero misleading claims.
- **Secure Role-Based Governance**: Production-grade Bcrypt password hashing, cryptographically random session tokens, and zeroized credentials.

By bridging the gap between raw global environmental datasets and strategic intelligence, EcoTwin provides a unified, transparent, and resilient ecosystem that empowers stakeholders, researchers, and government entities to run simulations, evaluate resilient policies, and formulate data-driven decisions for a sustainable future.
